1. Who we are

The Moi Liki app is developed by Artem Shelepun (individual), who is the controller of your personal data. Questions about your data: privacy@moiliki.app.

2. Data on your phone

The app works without an account. In that case everything you enter is stored only on your phone and is not sent anywhere:

  • medicines: name, strength, expiry date, batch, registration number, manufacturer, notes, stock;
  • cabinets, dosing schedules and dose history;
  • photos of packages.

The package scanner reads text from the photo on the phone itself (using iOS). The photo is never sent to our server, even with an account.

Face ID / Touch ID is checked by iOS; we receive no biometric data.

To suggest names, check batches and show leaflets, the app downloads public reference data from our server: the medicine catalogue, the list of banned batches and patient leaflets. These requests are anonymous: they do not carry the contents of your cabinet. Matching your batches against banned ones happens on the phone.

3. Account and sync

An account is only needed if you want to sync data between devices or keep a backup. You sign in with Apple, Google or a one-time code sent by email. There are no passwords.

When you sign in, we store:

  • your email address;
  • for Apple or Google sign-in, your account identifier at that provider and the email it shares (Apple may share a private relay address @privaterelay.appleid.com);
  • for Apple sign-in, an Apple token used to revoke access when you delete your account;
  • for email sign-in, a hash of the one-time code (never the code itself), valid for 10 minutes;
  • session tokens that keep you signed in on this device.

Synced to the server: medicines (without photos), cabinets, dosing schedules and dose history.

The server also keeps technical logs (IP address, time and path of the request) to protect against abuse and investigate errors. These logs are kept for a short time and are not linked to the contents of your cabinet.

4. Health data

The medicines you keep or take and your dosing schedule may reveal information about your health. Ukrainian data protection law (Article 7 of the Law on Personal Data Protection) and the GDPR (Article 9) treat such data as a special category. We handle it accordingly: we process it only to make the app work for you, never share it for advertising or analytics, and use it for no other purpose.

5. Purposes and legal basis

  • The app on your phone: this data never reaches us.
  • Sync and backup: to provide the service you turned on by signing in (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Sync starts only after you sign in.
  • Sign-in code email: so that you can sign in.
  • Technical logs: protecting the service from abuse (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

7. Where data is stored

The main database is on a server in the EU (Amsterdam, the Netherlands). Resend, GitHub, Apple and Google are US companies, so some data may be processed outside the EU; such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, depending on the provider.

8. Phone permissions

  • Notifications: dose and expiry reminders. They are scheduled on the phone itself; we do not send push notifications from our server.
  • Camera and photos: to take or pick a photo of a package.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: if you turn on the app lock.

Each permission can be turned off in iOS Settings; the app keeps working without the related feature.

9. How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you delete it or delete the app.
  • On the server: as long as your account exists.
  • Sign-in codes: valid for 10 minutes and removed from the database within a day after that.
  • Backups: 30 days; after you delete your account, your data disappears from them within 30 days.

10. Deletion

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. The server immediately deletes your email, linked Apple and Google sign-ins, and all synced medicines, cabinets, schedules and dose history; Apple access is revoked. The data on that phone is erased too. To delete your account without the app, see Delete your account.

Without an account, all data is on the phone only, and deleting the app removes it.

11. Your rights

You have the right to know what data about you we process, get a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing or object to it. Write to privacy@moiliki.app from your account’s email address; we reply within 30 days.

If you believe we are breaching your rights, you can complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or to the data protection authority in your EU country.

12. Children

The app is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Security

  • All connections to the server are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Sign-in codes are stored only as a hash.
  • Session tokens are kept in the phone’s secure storage (Keychain).
  • Only the developer and the hosting provider have access to the database.

14. This website

moiliki.app sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other servers; even the fonts are hosted here. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitors’ IP addresses for security and to run the service. The link to the Telegram channel leads to Telegram, where Telegram’s own terms apply.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We will warn you in the app in advance about significant changes, such as a new provider that would receive your data.
