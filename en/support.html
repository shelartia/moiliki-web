Support

A question, a bug or an idea: write to support@moiliki.app. We answer in Ukrainian or English, usually within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a medicine?

Tap “+” on the Medicines tab (Ліки). Start typing the name: the app suggests medicines from the national register and fills in the form and strength. You can also take a photo of the package to read the name, batch and expiry date from it.

How do reminders work?

Expiry reminders come in advance; dose reminders follow your schedule. They are scheduled on the phone itself, so they work offline too. To receive them, allow notifications for Moi Liki in iOS Settings.

What is a “batch check”?

Ukraine’s drug regulator bans individual batches of medicines for defects, counterfeits or a manufacturer recall. The app compares the batch number from your package with the list of banned batches and shows you if yours is on it. The batch number is printed on the box next to the expiry date.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully without signing in, and your data stays on your phone. An account is only for a backup and for moving to a new phone. For free, it is open on one device: signing in on a second one ends the session on the first, and the app warns you before that. Several devices at once come with the Moi Liki+ subscription.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

On the old phone, sign in (Profile → Sign in, in the app: Профіль → Увійти) and let it sync. On the new phone, install the app and sign in the same way: Apple, Google or a code sent to the same email. Your data comes across automatically, except package photos: they are stored only on the phone.
