Every medicine at home, under control

Moi Liki reminds you before medicines expire and when a dose is due, and warns you if Ukraine’s drug regulator bans a batch you keep at home.

Three reasons to install it

Recalled batches

Ukraine’s drug regulator bans batches for defects or counterfeits. The app checks the batches in your cabinet against the register and warns you if yours is one of them.

Expiry dates

See what is about to expire. Reminders come early: a month ahead, a week ahead and on the last day.

Doses on schedule

A reminder for every dose, one tap to mark it taken, and a history of how well you kept to the schedule.

Sound familiar?

  • A headache at 2 a.m., and nobody knows what’s in the medicine box or whether it has expired.
  • You heard about a banned batch and can’t tell whether it’s the one in your kitchen drawer.
  • The medicine turned out to be expired just when you needed it.
  • You can’t remember whether you already took the evening pill.

And also

The national drug register

16,000 medicines, available offline. Start typing a name and the form and strength fill in for you.

Package scanner

Take a photo of the box: the name, batch and expiry date are read from it.

Leaflets and alternatives

The official patient leaflet for a medicine, and alternatives with the same active ingredient.

Several cabinets

Home, car, country house, travel: medicines where they actually are.

No account, no ads, no tracking

The app works straight away, without signing up, and your medicines stay on your phone. An account is only for sync and backup: sign in with Apple, Google or a code sent by email, with no passwords.
